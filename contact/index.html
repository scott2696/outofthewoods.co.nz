Contact — frequently asked questions

How quickly will you reply?

Two working days for most enquiries, and usually faster for corrections. We are a small team in New Zealand, so replies arrive during NZ business hours.

I have a dispute with a casino. Can you help?

We cannot adjudicate — we are a publisher, not a regulator or an alternative dispute resolution body. What we can do is tell you which regulator the operator is licensed by and how to escalate, log the complaint against that operator’s trust score, and follow up with the operator ourselves where it is one we list. Email complaints@outofthewoods.co.nz with the operator name, dates, amounts and what the operator has told you.

I think a figure on your site is wrong.

Please tell us. Email editor@outofthewoods.co.nz with the URL and what is wrong. We aim to correct within five working days and we note material corrections on the page.

I represent an operator and want to be listed.

Email editor@outofthewoods.co.nz. Listing follows testing and scoring, not commercial discussion: we will open an account, deposit, play, withdraw and score you against the same published model as everyone else. We do not sell placement, we do not send drafts for approval, and we do not remove findings.

Do you accept guest posts or link placements?

No. We do not publish guest contributions, sponsored posts, paid links or link insertions, and we do not reply to outreach offering them.

How do I request my personal data or ask for it to be deleted?

Email privacy@outofthewoods.co.nz. Under the Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to access and correct personal information we hold about you, and we will respond within 20 working days. See our privacy policy.

If you need gambling support, not us. Gambling Helpline Aotearoa is free, confidential and available 24 hours on 0800 654 655, or free text 8006. See our responsible gambling page for the full list of New Zealand services.
