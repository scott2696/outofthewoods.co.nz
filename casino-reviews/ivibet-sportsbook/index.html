How long do withdrawals take at Ivibet Sportsbook?

Ivibet Sportsbook states 2–12 hours on crypto, 12–24 hours on e-wallets and 2–5 business days on cards, with a weekly cap of NZ$5,000 / week. We have not yet completed enough timed withdrawals here to publish a median, and we would rather say that than quote a single result as a benchmark.
