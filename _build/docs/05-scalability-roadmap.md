Build these only for the six operators that actually convert. Brand long-tail is

**Comparison pages are the highest-value sub-type** and are currently absent
from the whole NZ market: "Spinjo vs Kingdom", "Kingdom vs Smash",
"Rooster Bet vs Bet&Play". Genuine decision-stage intent, near-zero competition.

## Cross-linking strategy as the site grows

1. **Silo integrity.** Payment spokes link up to `/casino-payment-methods/` and across
   to at most two sibling rails. Game spokes link up to `/online-pokies/`.
   Never let a spoke link directly to a spoke in a different silo.
2. **Category pages become hubs.** As spokes are added, each category page gains
   a card grid linking to its children — this is the main distribution mechanism
   for new pages.
3. **Reviews are the crossroads.** Every review links to the categories the
   operator is strongest on, which means new category pages gain 19 contextual
   inbound links the day they launch.
4. **The trust pages stay universally linked.** `/how-we-rate-casinos/`, `/authors/`
   and `/responsible-gambling/` are linked from every page, permanently.
5. **Data pages earn the external links**, then pass authority inward. Link the
   payout report to `/fast-payout-casinos/`, the RTP index to
   `/casino-payout-percentages/`, the margin tracker to `/online-betting/`.
