## 3. Real user questions and pain points

### Method, stated honestly
- **Trustpilot** — read in full. Review pages for NZ-facing operators were
  accessible and are the source of the verbatim quotations and the complaint
  categorisation.
- **People Also Ask / common queries** — mined through repeated question-shaped
  searches and by extracting every competitor FAQ verbatim (casinos.com 10 Qs,
  bettingtop10 7 Qs, casino.org 4).
- **AnswerThePublic-style question expansion** — done manually from the question
  stems that recur across the SERP (what / why / can I / is it / how long / what
  happens if).
- **Reddit — NOT accessed.** reddit.com is blocked to this tool's user agent and
  general search did not surface thread content. The Reddit-specific pain points
  in this document are therefore *absent*, not synthesised. If you want them,
  they need a manual pass or a scraping tool with Reddit access. I would rather
  flag the hole than invent quotes.

## 6. Google Trends

**Queried directly** via the Google Trends NZ RSS feed (`geo=NZ`), September 2026.

Top NZ daily search trends at time of writing: **warriors game · john farnham ·
ufc · ufc today · epl results · stuff · metservice · dp world tour · bbc news**.
Australia's list (`geo=AU`) was similarly sport-dominated: *warriors vs knights ·
sanfl grand final · vfl grand final · lachie neale*.

**What this tells us, honestly:** no gambling term appears in NZ daily trending
searches — which is normal, since daily trends surface news spikes rather than
steady commercial demand. What the feed *does* confirm is that **New Zealand's
current attention is overwhelmingly on sport, and specifically on the NRL
finals** (Warriors) plus UFC, EPL and the DP World Tour.

**Trend angles implemented on the page:**
1. **Crash games / Aviator** — the genuine category trend in this vertical
   (~35% of mobile sessions, 77m+ MAU, 121 new titles in 2025). Given its own H3
   and threaded into the "best for" grid via Fortune Play and Spino.
2. **The licensing auction** — the live news hook. The DIA auction was scheduled
   for the end of September 2026, so it leads "What changed this month".
3. **Sport-led seasonality** — the Trends data supports pushing casino↔sportsbook
   crossover now (Rooster Bet, MadCasino) and is a stronger argument for
   prioritising the betting cluster in Q4. Noted in the roadmap, not forced onto
   this page.

**Limitation:** the RSS feed gives daily trending searches only. Trends *Explore*
(interest-over-time, related queries, rising terms for a specific keyword)
requires the JS app and was not accessible. Validate seasonality for "online
casino NZ" manually in Trends Explore before planning a content calendar.
